# Product

## Register

product

## Users

The primary users are learners who open the web app to access assigned video courses, continue lessons, and understand what they have completed. They are usually in a task-focused learning session, moving between course lists, course details, and video playback with minimal tolerance for visual noise.

Secondary workflows currently exist for course and video creation, but future design decisions should treat learner continuity as the default priority unless a task explicitly targets creator or admin surfaces.

## Product Purpose

This product helps learners watch assigned course content with clear navigation, reliable video playback, and visible progress. Success means a learner can quickly find the right course, resume the right video, understand completion state, and recover from empty, loading, unavailable, or unprocessed video states without confusion.

## Brand Personality

Focused, calm, practical.

The interface should feel like a quiet learning workspace: direct, steady, and easy to scan. It should support concentration around video content and course progress rather than competing for attention.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic SaaS dashboards, decorative gradients, vague hero metrics, predictable blue-card layouts, and visually busy course marketplaces. Do not make the learner experience feel like a marketing landing page, a playful classroom game, or an admin console unless the specific route requires that mode.

## Design Principles

- Put learning continuity first.
- Make progress and state obvious.
- Keep the interface quiet around video content.
- Prefer direct workflows over decorative surfaces.
- Make empty, loading, and unavailable states useful.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use WCAG AA as the baseline for future design work. Preserve strong text contrast, keyboard-accessible controls, visible focus states, responsive layouts, and reduced-motion support. Do not rely on color alone to communicate course status, completion, upload state, or playback availability.
